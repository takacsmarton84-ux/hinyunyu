How to play

  • Tap Play, then touch the screen to start.
  • Drag left and right anywhere on the screen to steer the UFO.
  • Dodge the meteors: the first hit ends the run.
  • Catch coins for bonus points: bronze 50, silver 150, gold 500.
  • You also get 10 points for every second you survive.

FAQ

Where is my leaderboard stored?

Only on your iPhone. There is no online leaderboard and no account.

How do I change the difficulty?

Open Settings from the main menu. Each difficulty has its own best score and leaderboard.

How do I turn off sound or vibration?

In Settings you can switch sound effects, music and vibration on or off separately.

The game stopped when I left the app.

That's on purpose: the game pauses automatically. Tap the screen to continue.

How do I reset my scores?

Delete and reinstall the app. This removes all scores stored on the device.
