Add to Home Screen

Put Hinyunyu Space Race on your Home Screen to play it like an app: full screen, and even without internet.

  1. 1
    Tap the Share button

    It's in the bar at the bottom of Safari. Don't see it? Tap ••• first.

  2. 2
    Tap “Add to Home Screen”

    Scroll down the list if you can't see it straight away.

  3. 3
    Tap “Add”

    It's in the top right corner.

  4. 4
    Open it from your Home Screen

    Look for this icon and tap it to play.

Using Chrome on iPhone? Tap the Share button in the address bar, then follow steps 2–4.
