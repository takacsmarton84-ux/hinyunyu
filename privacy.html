Privacy Policy

Hinyunyu Space Race · Last updated: 19 October 2026

Hinyunyu Space Race is an offline arcade game for iPhone. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it. In short: nothing leaves your iPhone.

Data we collect

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data. The app has no account or sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Data stored on your device

To work, the app saves the following on your iPhone only:

  • your best score and local leaderboard (score, survival time and date of your top runs),
  • your settings (difficulty, sound effects, music, vibration).

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time by deleting the app. It may be included in your own iCloud or device backups, which are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data at all, it does not collect data from children.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, ads, or analytics services.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Email tizenhet17@outlook.com.

Adatvédelmi tájékoztató (magyar)

A Hinyunyu Space Race semmilyen személyes adatot nem gyűjt, nem tárol és nem oszt meg. Nincs fiók, nincs analitika, nincs reklám, nincs követés, és az app nem csatlakozik az internethez.

Az app csak a telefonodon tárolja a rekordodat, a helyi ranglistát (pontszám, túlélt idő, dátum) és a beállításaidat. Ezeket soha nem küldi el senkinek; az app törlésével bármikor törölheted őket.
